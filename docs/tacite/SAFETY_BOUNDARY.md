# Tacite safety and medical boundary

## Boundary statement

Tacite does not give medical advice.

It does not:

- diagnose
- recommend medication
- suggest increasing medication
- suggest reducing medication
- suggest stopping medication
- suggest starting medication
- claim that a medication caused a symptom
- claim that a medication solved a symptom
- replace a psychiatrist, psychologist, GP, therapist, clinician, or emergency service
- act as an AI therapist

It may say:

- Here is what you recorded.
- Here is a timeline.
- Here is a summary you may want to discuss with a professional.
- This may be worth bringing up with a specialist.

## Suggested user-facing wording

If you are wondering whether you need a psychiatrist or psychologist, it may be worth speaking to one.

Tacite is not medical advice. It can help you make what is happening more concrete, put things into words, and prepare what to say to an actual specialist.

## Safety-critical content

If the user records safety-critical content, Tacite should not try to handle the issue itself.

Safety-critical content includes:

- suicidal thoughts
- self-harm urges
- self-harm behavior
- feeling unsafe
- sudden severe agitation
- possible mania/hypomania markers
- psychotic symptoms
- severe allergic reaction
- chest pain
- fainting
- dangerous palpitations
- severe confusion
- other urgent physical or psychiatric danger

## Safety response principle

Tacite should show a clear message directing the user to emergency services, crisis support, or a qualified professional.

It must not:

- give crisis counseling as a chatbot
- negotiate safety
- recommend medication changes
- minimize risk
- ask endless follow-up questions before showing help
- hide urgent help behind menus

## Language rules

Use:

- You recorded...
- You noted...
- You described...
- This may be worth bringing up with a professional.
- If you may be in immediate danger, contact emergency services now.

Avoid:

- You have...
- This means...
- You should take...
- You should stop...
- This medication caused...
- This treatment failed...
