# Tacite privacy and security risks

## Data sensitivity

Tacite may store highly sensitive data:

- mental-health symptoms
- medication names or treatment labels
- dose changes
- suicidal thoughts
- self-harm urges
- sexual side effects
- trauma-related notes
- work or relationship context
- names of clinicians
- appointment notes
- identifying life events

Treat all records as sensitive.

## Main risks

- raw notes accidentally logged
- AI payload leakage
- overclaiming anonymity
- mandatory email undermining privacy positioning
- local device exposure
- forgotten exports
- insecure backups
- unclear deletion
- unclear retention
- unauthorized access to another user's records
- unsafe crisis handling
- analytics capturing sensitive content
- third-party subprocessors touching health-related data

## Mitigations

- no real name required
- avoid saying anonymous if sync/account recovery exists
- no raw notes in logs
- no AI by default
- no AI before legal/privacy review for public users
- route ownership checks
- encrypted local storage
- export/delete rights
- retention policy
- HDS hosting review
- subprocessor review
- no ads
- no selling user data
- no content analytics on raw notes
- clear safety boundary

## Legal and hosting review required before launch

Before public launch, review:

- hosting provider HDS scope
- whether Tacite falls under health-data hosting obligations
- GDPR legal basis
- explicit consent flow if needed
- processor agreements
- AI provider terms and data handling
- data residency
- retention and deletion
- user export
- breach process
- DPIA need
- privacy policy
- terms of use
- medical/safety disclaimer

## Product wording risk

Avoid:

- anonymous
- medical record
- diagnosis
- clinical score
- treatment recommendation
- AI therapist
- naming the generic summary feature after a clinician role

Prefer:

- private
- no real name required
- summary
- summary so far
- appointment summary
- subjective snapshot
- treatment label
- what you recorded
