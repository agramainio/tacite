# Tacite UX flows

## UX principle

Tacite should start from the user's purpose, not from diagnosis, identity, or a questionnaire.

The first experience must reduce cognitive load and get the user to a useful first action quickly.

## First launch flow

1. Welcome and safety boundary
2. Purpose choice
3. Timeline beginning
4. Optional starting snapshot
5. First capture
6. Timeline

## Welcome screen

Copy direction:

Tacite

Record what changed. Prepare what to say.

This is a private place to remember what happened, put it into words, and prepare what to say to a professional.

Tacite does not give medical advice or diagnosis.

Actions:

- I am already in care
- I am preparing for care
- Just record something

## Purpose choices

### Already in care

For users who are seeing a professional or managing treatment changes.

Helps record:

- treatment changes
- dose changes
- side effects
- symptoms
- appointments
- questions
- functioning changes
- what changed since last appointment

### Preparing for care

For users who are not yet seeing anyone, are waiting for care, or do not know how to explain what is wrong.

Helps record:

- what feels wrong
- when it started
- what it affects
- what makes it worse
- what helps
- what they want help with
- what feels hard or embarrassing to say

### Just record something

For users who do not want setup.

The app should allow immediate capture, then gently organize later.

## Timeline beginning flow

Question:

Where should this timeline begin?

Options:

- Today
- When this started
- When treatment changed
- Last appointment
- I am not sure

Signup date must not be treated as the real beginning of the story.

Backdated entries must always be supported.

## Starting snapshot flow

Prompt:

Save a quick picture of how things feel now?

This helps compare later changes.
It is not a diagnosis or clinical score.
You can skip it.

Actions:

- Quick snapshot
- Skip for now
- Just record something

Suggested dimensions:

- Mood
- Anxiety
- Sleep
- Energy
- Focus
- Starting tasks

Optional:

- Body / side effects
- Appetite
- Social contact
- Work/study
- Sexual side effects
- Emotional numbness

## Capture flow

Preferred V0 capture screen:

Record something

Treatment:

- Start treatment label
- Change dose
- Stop treatment label
- Missed/late dose

Experience:

- Mood/anxiety
- Sleep
- Focus/tasks
- Side effect
- Functioning

Appointment:

- Question
- Thing to mention
- Summary note

Other:

- Free note

## AI-off flow

AI-off must not be a blank note field.

AI-off uses:

- quick cards
- chips
- rough subjective labels
- optional text
- one-tap record to timeline
- template summaries

## AI-on flow

AI-on can interpret messy notes.

Flow:

1. User enters messy note
2. AI produces one compact interpretation
3. User chooses:
   - Looks right
   - Edit
   - Keep raw only
4. Confirmed result goes to timeline

No multi-card confirmation.

## Discarded UX

Do not use:

- Tinder-style swipe/card-deck confirmation
- messenger/chatbot interface
- long questionnaire onboarding
- forced daily logging
- streaks
- overdue counters

## Swipeable card rule

Timeline cards may be swipeable for contextual actions.

Swipe actions are shortcuts only.

Every swipe action must have a visible non-swipe alternative for accessibility.

Good actions:

- edit
- change date
- change type
- move to another episode
- add to summary
- remove from summary
- mark as question
- mark as side effect
- archive / hide
- show original note
