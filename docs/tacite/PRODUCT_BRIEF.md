# Tacite product brief

## Product name

Tacite

Planned URL:

tacite.resfactae.xyz

## One-line description

Tacite is a private memory and preparation tool for recording what changed, understanding the timeline later, and preparing a clearer summary to bring to care.

## Core promise

Record what changed. Prepare what to say.

## Product position

Tacite helps users record, structure, and later explain what has been happening to them, especially when mental-health symptoms, treatment changes, side effects, fatigue, anxiety, depression, attention problems, stress, shame, or poor memory make the story hard to reconstruct.

Tacite is not:

- a medication reminder
- a productivity app
- an AI therapist
- a chatbot therapist
- a diagnostic tool
- a treatment recommender
- a clinical scoring product
- a replacement for a psychiatrist, psychologist, GP, therapist, clinician, or emergency service

Tacite is:

- a private timeline
- a memory aid
- a preparation tool
- a structured record of what changed
- a way to preserve messy notes and later turn them into a clearer story
- a way to generate an editable summary from a selected range

## Practical copy direction

Use:

- Record what changed. Prepare what to say.
- What changed, when, and what to mention.
- A private timeline for treatment changes, symptoms, side effects, functioning, context, and questions.
- Avoid leaving the appointment thinking: I forgot to mention that.
- Noter ce qui change. Préparer ce qu’il faut dire.
- Annotare cosa cambia. Preparare cosa dire.

Avoid:

- healing journey
- unlock your inner voice
- silent suffering
- AI therapist
- wellness companion
- mood optimizer
- productivity for mental health
- medical journal
- diagnosis assistant

## Product law

If Tacite becomes only a text note field with login, it fails.

The note is only the input.

The product is:

- chronological timeline
- structured records
- before/after memory
- functioning and side-effect context
- questions to bring up
- range-based summary
