# V0 scope

## V0 includes

- Account with no real name required.
- Email or one-time-code authentication.
- Treatment episode thread.
- Appointment-preparation thread.
- Messy note capture.
- Emoji capture and cautious interpretation.
- Original note preservation.
- AI formatting/summarization as explicit opt-in only.
- Manual entry mode when AI is off.
- Timeline of confirmed entries.
- Event-based check-ins.
- Plain text appointment summary export.
- Basic PDF export if simple enough.
- Safety interruption for crisis or urgent medical markers.
- Export and delete options.

## V0 excludes

- Medication reminders.
- Smartwatch integration.
- HealthKit.
- Health Connect.
- Live doctor sharing.
- Calendar sync.
- Social features.
- Collaboration.
- AI therapist chat.
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendation.
- Dose recommendation.
- Full analytics dashboard.
- Daily streaks.
- Habit tracking.
- Gamification.
- Ads.
- Selling data.
- Public beta with real health records before review.

## Non-negotiable V0 principles

- Skip is normal.
- No shame.
- No overdue language.
- No streaks.
- No diagnosis.
- No medical advice.
- No hidden AI.
- No raw notes in logs.
- No production health data outside approved hosting.
