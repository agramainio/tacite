# Product brief

## Working name

interim

## Problem statement

The app addresses the difficulty many people have in remembering, organizing, and clearly explaining mental-health experiences over time.

This difficulty can become worse when symptoms, treatment changes, side effects, stress, fatigue, anxiety, depression, attention problems, or shame make the story hard to reconstruct later.

The app is designed for two overlapping situations:

1. People who know that something is wrong but struggle to put it into words.
2. People whose medication, therapy, or treatment context is changing and who need to remember what changed, why it changed, how they felt before and after, what improved, what worsened, and what they should mention to a specialist.

The app’s purpose is not to diagnose, give medical advice, or replace a psychiatrist, psychologist, GP, therapist, or emergency service.

It is a private memory and preparation tool. It helps users quickly capture messy notes, preserve their original wording, structure events into a clear timeline, and generate an editable summary they can bring to an appointment.

## Core promise

You do not have to remember everything later.

You do not have to explain it perfectly on the spot.

The app helps you turn messy notes into a clear summary you can bring to a specialist.

## Product category

Private memory and appointment-preparation tool.

Not a therapy product.

Not a treatment recommendation product.

Not a medication-management product.

Not an emergency-support product.

## Primary users

- People preparing to speak with a psychiatrist, psychologist, GP, therapist, or other specialist.
- People changing medication, dose, therapy, or treatment context.
- People who struggle to reconstruct how they felt over time.
- People who freeze during appointments and forget what they wanted to say.
- People who need a calm place to record symptoms, side effects, functioning, and questions.

## Core object

The main object is not the medication.

The main object is the thread of change.

A thread may be:

- a treatment episode
- an appointment-preparation thread
- a therapy-preparation thread
- a difficult-period memory thread

## V0 product test

V0 succeeds if a user can:

1. Create a private account without using a real name.
2. Create a treatment or appointment-preparation thread.
3. Add a messy note.
4. Preserve the original note.
5. Optionally ask AI to format the note.
6. Confirm, edit, or reject the AI draft.
7. See confirmed entries on a timeline.
8. Export a clear appointment summary.
